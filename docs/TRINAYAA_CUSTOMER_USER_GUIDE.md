## 2. SHOPPING CART, PROMOTIONS & CHECKOUT

### Adding Items to Cart
1. Select your preferred pack size (50g, 100g, or 250g) and quantity.
2. Click **Add to Cart**.
3. View cart summary at any time by clicking the shopping bag icon in the top header.

### Free Shipping & Pan-India Delivery
- **Orders ₹799 and above:** **FREE Pan-India Delivery** automatically applied.
- **Orders below ₹799:** Standard flat shipping fee of ₹60.

### Festival Coupons & Discounts
- At the checkout screen, active promotional banners and festival coupon cards are displayed directly above the promo code box.
- Click **Apply** on any eligible festival offer, or enter your personal promo code.

### Flexible Delivery & Billing Options
- **Separate Billing & Shipping:** Purchasing for family or clients? Uncheck *"Billing address same as shipping address"* to enter a distinct billing address for your GST/tax invoice.
- **Gift Delivery:** Check *"This order is a gift"*, enter the recipient's name, and add a personalized gift message (up to 500 characters). We will include your message on a handwritten card and omit commercial prices from the package exterior.

### Payment Methods
- **UPI / QR:** Instant payments via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM.
- **Debit / Credit Cards & Net Banking:** Secure processing powered by Razorpay.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD):** Available across India for eligible botanical items.

## 4. NEW PRODUCT ENQUIRIES (CAN'T FIND AN HERB?)

If you are looking for a rare traditional herb, root, or specific seed not currently listed in our 162-product catalogue:
1. Navigate to **Account -> New Product Enquiry** (or click **Bulk & Sourcing Enquiry**).
2. Enter the herb name (in English or Tamil), preferred quantity, and specific sourcing requirements.
3. Our botanical procurement team in Erode will evaluate the harvest season and contact you with availability and pricing.

## 5. ORDER TRACKING & EASY RETURNS

### Tracking Your Order
1. Log into your account and open **My Orders & Tracking**.
2. Click on any order to view real-time transit status, courier carrier, and tracking number.
3. You will also receive SMS and WhatsApp tracking notifications once your parcel is dispatched from our Erode hub.

### Hassle-Free Returns
1. If your order arrives with broken seals or transit damage, open your order details within 7 days of delivery.
2. Click **Request Return / Replacement**.
3. Select the affected item, choose the reason, provide a short description, and submit.
4. An authoritative RMA tracking number (`TRN-RMA-YYYY-XXXXX`) is generated immediately. Our team will review within 24 hours to arrange pickup and prompt replacement or refund.
